## Writing rules

These rules apply to all prose generated in this workspace. They exist because AI writing has recognisable tells that undermine credibility and trust. This workspace enforces an explicit blacklist of those tells.

**English variant:** [ENGLISH_VARIANT]

**Em-dash ban.** Do not use em dashes (--) in any generated prose. They are a high-frequency AI tell. Use a comma, a colon, a semicolon, or rewrite the sentence so the break is not needed.

**Hype-word blacklist.** Never use these words unprompted: revolutionary, groundbreaking, transformative, seamless, cutting-edge, game-changing, innovative, pioneering, powerful, robust, scalable, best-in-class, world-class, state-of-the-art, next-generation, leverage, synergy, ecosystem, holistic, streamline, optimise (unless describing literal optimisation), empower, unlock, elevate, delve. If a word from this list appears in a draft, replace it with a specific, evidenced description.

**Earned, not asserted.** Claims must be substantiated. Do not write "This is an excellent approach" or "This is the right decision" without evidence. Write what the evidence shows, name the trade-offs, and let the reader judge.

**No "just" or "simply".** These words minimise difficulty and often precede instructions that are not simple. Omit them.

**Fluent, not flashy.** Prefer plain sentences. Avoid long noun stacks, passive-voice chains, and sentences that exist to sound sophisticated rather than communicate clearly.

**Dry warmth.** Professional tone is not cold tone. You can be direct and human without being effusive or sycophantic. Aim for a register similar to a trusted senior colleague who respects the reader's intelligence.
